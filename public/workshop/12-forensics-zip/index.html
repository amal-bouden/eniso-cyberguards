🗄️ The Locked Vault

Category: Misc / Password Cracking  |  Difficulty: Medium  |  Points: 250

📖 Description

During a forensics investigation, you found a suspicious password-protected ZIP archive on a suspect's laptop. The archive is named vault.zip and contains a file called flag.txt.

Alongside the archive, you also recovered two text files from the same directory. They seem to be leftover notes from the suspect. Piece together the password from the clues hidden in them.

The flag is base64-encoded inside flag.txt — decode it once you crack the archive.

💡 Hints

💡 Hint 1 — How do I find the password? (click to reveal)
Hint 1: Read both text files carefully. club_notes.txt gives you the format of the password. membership_card_scan.txt gives you the values to fill in. The club abbreviation and membership year are both visible.
💡 Hint 2 — What are the two-letter initials? (click to reveal)
Hint 2: "CyberGuards" — the membership card shows the club name. The two-letter abbreviation of CyberGuards is CG. The membership year shown on the card is 2024. Put it together with the @ separator from the format.
💡 Hint 3 — How do I open a password-protected ZIP? (click to reveal)
Hint 3: On Linux/Mac: unzip -P YOUR_PASSWORD vault.zip
On Windows: right-click → Extract All → enter password when prompted.
Or use Python:
import zipfile with zipfile.ZipFile("vault.zip") as z: data = z.read("flag.txt", pwd=b"YOUR_PASSWORD") print(data.decode())
💡 Hint 4 — I got the file content but it's not the flag yet (click to reveal)
Hint 4: The content of flag.txt is base64-encoded. Decode it:
import base64 print(base64.b64decode("PASTE_CONTENT_HERE").decode())
Or online: use CyberChef → From Base64.
